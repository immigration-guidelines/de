# Immigration guidance: Germany (federal level and states other than Berlin)

Published guidance that the authorities of Germany (federal level and states other than Berlin) issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 812 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- **Country-of-origin reports:** 871 files in `coi/`, with their own `coi/index.tsv`.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

**Berlin is in its own repository.** The Berlin immigration office's guidance (Verfahrenshinweise zum Aufenthalt in Berlin, VAB) is kept with its full version history, one commit per version since 2007, at [immigration-guidelines/berlin_vab](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/berlin_vab); the source PDFs are at [immigration-guidelines/vabpdfs](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/vabpdfs).

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
